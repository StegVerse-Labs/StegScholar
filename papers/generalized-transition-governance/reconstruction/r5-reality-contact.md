# GTG R5 — Reality-Contact Reconstruction

## Status

StegVerse research and integration mirror. This document does not claim canonical ownership, R5 completion, mathematical closure, empirical validity, certification, execution authority, release readiness, archive readiness, or universal admissibility.

## Objective

R5 asks whether a reconstructed governance claim remains connected to identified evidence of an external effect, observation, custody event, provider event, or physical-world state rather than only to an internally generated assertion, simulation, replay, or model state.

## Evidence classes

- `PROVIDER_RECEIPT` — a receipt emitted by the system or provider that performed or observed the bounded operation.
- `CUSTODY_RECEIPT` — evidence that an identified object, package, artifact, or record changed custody under a declared chain.
- `HUMAN_OBSERVATION` — a bounded observation tied to an identified observer, time, scope, and observation method.
- `PHYSICAL_MEASUREMENT` — a measurement tied to an identified instrument, calibration or validation reference, time, and subject.
- `EXTERNAL_STATE_ATTESTATION` — a signed or otherwise integrity-bound statement about an external system state.
- `INTERNAL_SIMULATION` — model-generated, test-harness, mocked, emulated, or hypothetical evidence.
- `ASSERTED_ONLY` — a claim lacking independently addressable evidence.

## Provenance requirements

Every R5 evidence object must identify:

1. evidence class;
2. evidence identifier;
3. source system or observer;
4. subject or effect observed;
5. observation or event time;
6. collection method;
7. integrity reference;
8. custody or transport history where applicable;
9. freshness boundary;
10. the exact claim the evidence supports.

## Exclusions

The following cannot produce `R5_PASS` by themselves:

- deterministic replay;
- internal logs generated by the same reasoning process that makes the claim;
- simulations, mocks, fixtures, or synthetic provider responses;
- unsigned screenshots without preserved source and custody context;
- stale evidence outside its declared freshness boundary;
- evidence whose subject is detached from the claimed external effect;
- human statements with no declared observation scope;
- provider receipts that prove request acceptance but not the claimed resulting effect.

## Bounded outcomes

- `R5_PASS` — each required reality-contact claim is supported by current, integrity-bound, subject-matched external evidence, and no material contradiction remains unresolved.
- `R5_PARTIAL` — useful external evidence exists, but freshness, custody, subject matching, completeness, or contradiction resolution is incomplete.
- `R5_FAIL_CLOSED` — the claim depends only on internal evidence, the evidence is stale or detached, required custody is absent, a material contradiction is unresolved, or any authority escalation is attempted.
- `R5_NOT_TESTED` — no bounded reality-contact package has yet been evaluated.

## Promotion rules

1. `INTERNAL_SIMULATION` and `ASSERTED_ONLY` never promote above `R5_FAIL_CLOSED` for a claim requiring external reality contact.
2. A provider receipt must prove the claimed effect, not merely request submission or workflow initiation.
3. Human observation requires declared observer identity, scope, method, and time.
4. Physical measurement requires an instrument and validation or calibration reference appropriate to the claim.
5. Stale evidence cannot be silently refreshed by replay.
6. Contradictory external evidence remains visible and blocks `R5_PASS` until resolved through a declared rule.
7. R5 evidence cannot create execution authority, certification authority, legal standing, or governance authority.
8. StegVerse mirrors and tests these distinctions but does not replace canonical GTG ownership.
