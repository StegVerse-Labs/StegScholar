# R4–R5 Subject-Identity Continuity

A transition does not survive merely because authority is valid, evidence is fresh, and both overlap in time. The subject named by the authority record must be demonstrably continuous with the subject represented by the reality-contact evidence and the transition candidate.

## Required bindings

A surviving boundary requires:

1. one canonical authority subject identifier;
2. one observed subject identifier;
3. an explicit, admissible binding between them;
4. binding validity at the transition boundary;
5. no unresolved identity contradiction, reassignment, alias collision, or detached evidence;
6. matching action and scope after identity resolution.

Names, labels, addresses, account handles, device identifiers, and organizational roles are not interchangeable merely because they appear similar. Consent does not repair a missing authority-subject binding. Standing does not prove observed-subject identity. Temporal overlap does not prove identity continuity.

## Outcomes

- `SUBJECT_CONTINUITY_SURVIVES`: the authority subject, observed subject, and transition subject are bound by complete, current, contradiction-free evidence.
- `SUBJECT_CONTINUITY_PARTIAL`: a plausible binding exists, but a required provenance or alias-history element is incomplete.
- `SUBJECT_CONTINUITY_FAIL_CLOSED`: the binding is absent, contradictory, reassigned, expired, detached, or attempts to upgrade an alias into authority.

This layer validates identity continuity only. It grants no execution authority, certification authority, release readiness, archive readiness, mathematical closure, or complete independent verification.
