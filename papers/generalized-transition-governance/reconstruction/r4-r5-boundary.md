# GTG R4→R5 Boundary Reconstruction

This StegVerse layer tests whether a reconstructed authority posture at R4 remains admissible when bound to R5 reality-contact evidence.

## Rule

An R4 authority result cannot survive the boundary merely because its delegation chain is structurally valid. The claimed action, subject, scope, time, and observed reality state must also remain mutually consistent.

## Outcomes

- `BOUNDARY_SURVIVES`: R4 authority is current and scope-matched, and R5 evidence is observed, fresh, subject-bound, custody-preserved, and non-contradictory.
- `BOUNDARY_PARTIAL`: authority is structurally valid but reality contact is incomplete, stale, or missing custody.
- `BOUNDARY_FAIL_CLOSED`: authority is invalid, the evidence concerns another subject or action, observations contradict the claim, or the case attempts to convert evidence into execution authority.

## Non-effects

This layer does not create execution authority, certification authority, canonical GTG R5 completion, independent verification, release readiness, archive readiness, or mathematical closure.
