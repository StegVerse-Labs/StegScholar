# Recoverability Geometry and the Rigel Number

**Author:** Rigel Randolph  
**Status:** Working research manuscript; formalism under validation  
**Repository:** StegVerse-Labs/StegScholar

## Purpose

This paper develops a candidate dimensionless interaction measure for systems that observe, infer, commit, and act upon changing reality under finite latency and uncertainty.

The research combines:

- a G-C-E simplex for governance, constraints, and execution;
- decomposed interaction latency `alpha_o + alpha_i + alpha_r`;
- a recoverability margin;
- a candidate Rigel number `Ri`;
- cross-domain comparisons with reachability, resilience, synchronization, and replicator dynamics.

## Files

- `manuscript.md` — canonical text manuscript.
- `validation-protocol.md` — falsification and empirical-testing plan.

## Scientific posture

This repository does not claim that the Rigel number is already a universal law or that the framework derives fundamental physical constants. Those are research questions requiring dimensional derivation, simulation, real data, and independent review.

## Next milestone

Produce a reproducible delayed-control simulation and determine whether:

1. observed failure occurs near the predicted `Ri = 1` boundary;
2. decomposed latency improves prediction beyond total latency alone;
3. the result remains stable under changes in noise, controller design, and recoverability definition.
