## Research questions

1. What wired, wireless, minimally invasive, and non-invasive interfaces can currently produce useful neural READ evidence?
2. What technologies can produce bounded neural WRITE/stimulation effects, at what targeting resolution and with what safety evidence?
3. Which systems are truly bidirectional versus a READ decoder paired with a separate effector?
4. What empirical evidence exists for target localization, calibration stability, bandwidth, latency, noise, spatial/temporal resolution, chronic use, and post-consequence observation?
5. Where do decoder priors, language models, shared autonomy, or task context contribute information not established by neural measurement alone?
6. What evidence distinguishes attempted speech, internal speech, perceived language, motor intention, semantic reconstruction, and actual authorization/consent?
7. Which current device architectures are tethered/wired, wireless, partially implanted, fully implanted, endovascular/endocisternal, surface cortical, intracortical, or non-invasive?
8. Which claims in StegNeuro's physical model are supported, challenged, or still unsupported by empirical evidence?

## Initial modality coverage

- intracortical microelectrode arrays;
- high-density ECoG/surface arrays;
- endovascular/endocisternal interfaces;
- wired and wireless implanted systems;
- EEG and MEG;
- fMRI semantic reconstruction (comparison/control modality);
- functional ultrasound READ;
- focused-ultrasound WRITE/neuromodulation;
- bidirectional stimulation/recording;
- speech and inner-speech decoding;
- chronic independent use;
- datasets relevant to non-invasive speech decoding.

## Claim boundary

The registry is supporting documentation and a discovery surface, not proof that StegNeuro has a working device. Each source must remain bounded to its studied participants, task, modality, hardware, and experimental conditions. Decoder performance does not establish intent, authorization, consent, or semantic identity.

## Refresh condition

Refresh when:

- a new StegNeuro device/adapter claim is proposed;
- a new BCI modality is added;
- a cited source is corrected/retracted/superseded;
- a major primary study, clinical trial result, safety update, or regulatory decision appears;
- at least quarterly while STEGNEURO-001 remains active.
