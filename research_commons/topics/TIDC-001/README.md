## Witnessed hypothesis

General-purpose technologies may produce temporally clustered waves of scientific and mathematical discovery rather than smooth productivity increases.

This page is the Research Commons Wiki entry point for the first governed research exemplar. It must preserve the difference between the original hypothesis, later protocol choices, collected evidence, computation, interpretation, and accepted conclusions.
