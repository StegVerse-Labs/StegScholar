## Current reviewed baseline

Primary-source review on 2026-09-07 establishes:

1. The official May 2026 U.S. Counterterrorism Strategy identifies three major terror-group categories, including `Violent Left-Wing Extremists, including Anarchists and Anti-Fascists`.
2. The same strategy says national CT activities will prioritize identification and neutralization of violent secular political groups and will `map` them domestically, identify membership, and map ties to international organizations such as Antifa.
3. The strategy also says CT powers will not be used against Americans who simply disagree with the Administration and states the mission as identifying groups with intent and capability to plot attacks against Americans.
4. The September 22, 2025 Executive Order designates Antifa a domestic terrorist organization and directs agencies to investigate, disrupt, and dismantle illegal operations conducted by Antifa or persons claiming to act on its behalf.
5. NSPM-7 directs JTTFs to coordinate investigations of political violence and intimidation, including recruitment/radicalization, funders, organizations, and networks, while tying domestic-terrorism designation recommendations to 18 U.S.C. § 2331(5).
6. 18 U.S.C. § 2331(5) defines domestic terrorism through dangerous-to-human-life criminal acts plus specified coercive/governmental intent and a domestic territorial nexus.
7. 18 U.S.C. § 2339A criminalizes material support or resources when the provider knows or intends that the support be used in preparation for or carrying out enumerated federal crimes; it does not require an FTO designation.
8. In the Prairieland ICE Detention Center case, DOJ obtained convictions and guilty pleas for `providing material support to terrorists`. DOJ's own account says some defendants attended a peaceful daytime protest and reported security details before later alleged/established unlawful conduct. The case therefore requires defendant-by-defendant analysis; it does not by itself establish that peaceful protest alone is a terrorism offense.

## Research discipline

This lane MUST distinguish:

```text
protected belief
protected speech
peaceful protest
civil disobedience / nonviolent unlawful conduct
property crime
conspiracy
material support under §2339A
personally committed violence
terrorism definition / terrorism sentencing consequences
executive-branch ideological classification
```

No source may be summarized as proving that `peaceful protest alone = terrorism` unless a charging instrument, factual basis, verdict, sentencing finding, or appellate opinion supports that exact proposition.

Likewise, the absence of a standalone domestic-terrorism offense must not be used to minimize investigative, charging, sentencing, or collateral consequences available through other statutes.

## Source posture

Priority order:

1. statutes and Federal Register / White House presidential actions;
2. indictments, informations, plea agreements/factual resumes, jury instructions, judgments, appellate filings and opinions;
3. DOJ/FBI/DHS official releases and testimony;
4. contemporaneous local/national reporting with named attorneys and docket-specific facts;
5. advocacy/defense materials, explicitly labeled as such.

Government and defense sources are evidence of their respective positions, not neutral proof merely because they are primary participants.
