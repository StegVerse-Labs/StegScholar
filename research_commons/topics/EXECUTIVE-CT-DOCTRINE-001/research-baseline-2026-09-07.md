# Research Baseline — 2026-09-07

Topic: `EXECUTIVE-CT-DOCTRINE-001`
Status: INITIAL REVIEWED BASELINE — NOT A FINAL LEGAL CONCLUSION

## Why this lane exists

The May 2026 U.S. Counterterrorism Strategy expressly places `Violent Left-Wing Extremists, including Anarchists and Anti-Fascists` inside a three-part terror-group taxonomy. The strategy also describes mapping domestic membership and ties to Antifa while separately promising not to use counterterrorism powers against Americans who merely disagree politically.

The research question is therefore not only what the Executive Branch says, but how that language connects to actual investigative and criminal authorities and how those authorities have already been used in cases involving protest activity.

## Initial findings

### 1. `Anti-Fascists` is used as a counterterrorism category, but the 2026 strategy does not provide a dictionary-style definition of the term

The strategy identifies the category and later refers to mapping ties to `international organizations like Antifa`. It does not, in the reviewed text, define the boundary between ordinary opposition to fascism, anti-fascist activism, Antifa association, violent extremist affiliation, and criminal conduct.

This is a definitional gap, not proof that all anti-fascist belief is criminalized.

### 2. The strategy contains both expansive operational language and limiting language

Expansive language includes identifying and neutralizing violent secular political groups, mapping membership, and mapping international ties. Limiting language says CT powers will not be used against Americans who simply disagree and states the CT mission in terms of groups with intent and capability to plot attacks.

The legal significance depends on how agencies translate these statements into predicates, guidance, referrals, investigations, arrests, charges, and evidence.

### 3. The 2025 Executive Order and NSPM-7 create an earlier doctrinal chain

The September 22, 2025 Executive Order designates Antifa as a domestic terrorist organization and directs agencies to use applicable authorities against illegal operations by Antifa or persons claiming to act on its behalf.

NSPM-7 then directs JTTFs to investigate political violence and intimidation, including recruitment/radicalization, funders, organizations, and networks, and ties designation recommendations to the statutory definition in 18 U.S.C. §2331(5).

This chronology matters because the 2026 strategy is not isolated language; it follows an already activated Executive Branch policy architecture.

### 4. Federal criminal law remains element-based

18 U.S.C. §2331(5) requires dangerous-to-human-life criminal acts, specified coercive/governmental intent, and a domestic nexus for the statutory definition of domestic terrorism.

18 U.S.C. §2339A criminalizes material support when the defendant knows or intends that support be used in preparation for or carrying out specified federal offenses. Its definition of support includes services, training, communications equipment, personnel, transportation, and other resources.

Thus ideological labeling and criminal liability are not identical. The research problem is how ideology and association are used as evidence to establish or expand the factual theory around those statutory elements.

### 5. Prairieland is a real terrorism-related prosecution involving an anti-ICE protest context, but it must be analyzed defendant by defendant

DOJ obtained jury convictions and guilty pleas under a `providing material support to terrorists` theory in the Prairieland ICE Detention Center case. DOJ states that some defendants participated in a peaceful daytime protest and reported security details before the later violent episode. Other evidence described firearms, fireworks/explosives, reconnaissance, property damage, attempted murder, post-event assistance, or other conduct depending on the defendant.

The case therefore shows that protest activity and ideology can appear inside the government's terrorism narrative. It does **not**, on the currently reviewed record, prove that peaceful demonstration by itself supplied the criminal elements of §2339A.

That distinction is a priority research target, not a reason to dismiss the concern.

### 6. The most important unanswered question is the attribution chain

The lane will test how far the following chain travels in actual doctrine and cases:

```text
political belief / anti-fascist position
-> self-identification or government attribution
-> association/network evidence
-> investigative predicate
-> conspiracy/material-support theory
-> terrorism-related charge or sentencing consequence
```

Each arrow requires evidence. None will be assumed.

## Immediate research tasks

- Acquire charging documents, plea factual resumes, jury instructions, judgments, and appellate filings for Prairieland.
- Separate each defendant's peaceful protest activity from alleged/admitted criminal conduct.
- Search 2025–2026 federal cases for other uses of Antifa/anti-fascist terminology in indictments, affidavits, detention motions, sentencing memoranda, or judicial opinions.
- Map First Amendment precedent governing advocacy, association, ideological evidence, and material support.
- Determine whether the domestic-terrorist-organization designation has produced judicially recognized legal consequences beyond existing statutory authorities.
- Track arrests of peaceful demonstrators separately from charges, convictions, dismissals, acquittals, and civil findings.

## Preliminary conclusion

There is enough primary-source evidence to justify a dedicated research lane. There is **not yet** enough reviewed court-record evidence to responsibly state that peaceful protest alone is being charged as terrorism. The evidence does show an Executive Branch counterterrorism doctrine that expressly names `Anti-Fascists`, an Antifa designation and network-investigation policy, and at least one major federal terrorism-related prosecution whose factual narrative includes anti-ICE protest activity and anti-fascist/Antifa attribution.

The lane is therefore ACTIVE, with the core factual question still open rather than pre-decided.
