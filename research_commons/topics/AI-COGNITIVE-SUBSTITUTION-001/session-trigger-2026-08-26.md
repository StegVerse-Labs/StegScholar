## Research question recovered from the session

When does AI assistance function as cognitive amplification, and when does it substitute for human cognitive transitions that an educational or evaluative process intended the learner to perform?

## Session-derived hypothesis set

The conversation contributed the following bounded hypotheses and modeling ideas:

- `artifact_quality != human_cognitive_work`;
- a high-quality output can coexist with limited human reasoning during production;
- the potentially important failure mode is **unobserved cognitive substitution** rather than AI use by itself;
- a final artifact does not necessarily prove the learner traversed the reasoning states intended by an assignment;
- an illustrative human learning transition chain can be represented as `S0 -> S1 -> S2 -> S3 -> S4 -> S5`, where the states move from vague intuition through proposition, conflicting evidence, discrimination, revision, and defensible conclusion;
- AI may be used to force human reasoning transitions through counterexamples, alternatives, and demanded justification rather than bypassing those transitions;
- future evaluation may need provenance of reasoning transitions in addition to terminal output quality.

These statements are preserved as **research hypotheses/conceptual framing**, not scientific findings.
