# Research Commons Wiki

The Research Commons Wiki is the governed public knowledge surface for research artifacts that contributors explicitly choose to share with StegVerse.

It is not a dump of user work and it is not a claim that shared research is correct. It is a versioned, provenance-preserving index of reusable research components, disagreements, replications, negative results, methods, datasets, and computational evidence.

## Purpose

```text
consented research artifact
→ license and scope verification
→ provenance and integrity validation
→ admissibility review
→ Research Commons Wiki entry
→ governed discovery and reuse
→ lineage, citation, and value attribution
```

The wiki should reduce duplicated effort, expose conflicting findings, preserve negative results, support replication, improve research cost estimates, and allow later studies to reuse admissible prior work.

## Entry classes

- Research topics and witnessed questions
- Approved protocols
- Source ledgers
- Dataset descriptions and data dictionaries
- Reusable datasets where consent and licensing permit
- Mathematical and statistical methods
- Computation manifests and reproducibility receipts
- Findings and conclusion classifications
- Negative and inconclusive results
- Replications and failed replications
- Contradictions and unresolved disputes
- Retractions, corrections, and supersession records
- Cost and compute benchmarks

## Required boundaries

```text
shared != public unless explicitly authorized
shared != accepted as true
payment discount != ownership transfer
reuse permission != unrestricted relicensing
wiki publication != scientific validation
model agreement != consensus
absence of contradiction != confirmation
```

## Contributor controls

Every contribution must preserve:

- consent version and consent hash;
- allowed artifact classes;
- allowed uses;
- attribution choice;
- license or reuse terms;
- privacy exclusions;
- embargo or publication timing;
- prospective revocation rules;
- immutable record of what was previously disclosed;
- compensation, discount, or credit basis where applicable.

Identity and attribution remain separate choices and default to private unless the contributor explicitly selects otherwise.

## Knowledge posture

Each entry must declare one of the following postures:

```text
witnessed_question
proposal
protocol_candidate
approved_protocol
collected_evidence
computed_result
independently_validated_result
exploratory_finding
moderate_support
strong_support
contradicted
inconclusive
not_identifiable
retracted
superseded
```

The wiki must not collapse these into one undifferentiated article state.

## Relationship to StegScholar

StegScholar remains the scholarly custody repository. The Research Commons Wiki is a governed discovery and synthesis layer over eligible StegScholar records and other explicitly admitted research artifacts.

## Relationship to the Governed Research Engine

The Governed Research Engine may search the wiki during proposal design and cost planning. Reuse requires a separate admissibility determination against the new protocol, consent scope, license terms, provenance, and contamination risks.
