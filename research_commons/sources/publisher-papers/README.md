# Publisher Papers Collection

This collection indexes scholarly records originating in `GCAT-BCAT-Engine/Publisher` for governed discovery through the Research Commons Wiki.

## Source boundary

Publisher remains the authoritative publication source for its paper records and paper files. Research Commons stores discovery, classification, relationship, and reuse records; it does not silently replace Publisher custody or publication state.

```text
Publisher paper record
→ source and license verification
→ Research Commons entry
→ knowledge-posture classification
→ relation mapping
→ protocol-specific reuse review
```

## Reuse boundary

A Publisher record being indexed here does not make its claims validated or automatically admissible in later research. Each reuse requires:

- source integrity and current-version verification;
- license verification;
- knowledge-posture preservation;
- relevance and contamination review;
- protocol-specific admissibility;
- citation and lineage retention.
