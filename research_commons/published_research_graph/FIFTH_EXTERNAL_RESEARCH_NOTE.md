# Fifth external research ingestion

`Standing Still Is Not an Option: Alternative Baselines for Attainable Utility Preservation` (Eresheim, Kovac, Adrowitzer; CD-MAKE 2023; DOI `10.1007/978-3-031-40837-3_15`) is admitted under `RC-CTRL-001` only because it independently evaluates an already-relevant side-effect mitigation family rather than merely sharing concepts.

The peer-reviewed Springer proceedings record and the authors' public implementation record identify a concrete limitation in prior Attainable Utility Preservation (AUP): reliance on a no-op action as the baseline. The paper introduces four alternative baselines that do not require a no-op action and evaluates them on multiple AI Safety Gridworlds, reporting broader task coverage with only small performance losses.

This ingestion is bounded to empirical evaluation/refinement of AUP-style impact regularization and AI Safety Gridworlds usage. It does not establish that AUP is correct in general, does not replicate or invalidate the full Gridworlds paper, and does not create support for StegVerse IICT or any other StegVerse research.

Source custody remains with Springer Nature / the original authors. `authority_effect: NONE`.
