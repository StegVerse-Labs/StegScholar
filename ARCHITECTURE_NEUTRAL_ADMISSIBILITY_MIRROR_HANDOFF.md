## Goal

Formalize and falsify the proposition that architectural difference alone is not a sufficient StegVerse denial basis when a candidate independently satisfies the governing requirement under the same valid evidence standard.

## Scope

This task does not require acceptance of all architectures and does not weaken safety, evidence, authority, standing, policy, or commit-time requirements. It tests whether incumbent-path conformity is being used as a hidden substitute for a substantive governance basis.

## Required outputs

- candidate invariant and exact failure semantics;
- positive/negative fixtures separating architectural difference from substantive insufficiency;
- tests where two architectures satisfy the same governing requirement by different valid evidence paths;
- tests where a novel architecture legitimately fails because evidence, authority, safety, or policy requirements are not satisfied;
- integration recommendation for GTG/admissibility evaluation.

## Provenance

This is a StegVerse refinement derived from the Millings Method external-framework comparison while preserving independent authorship and non-equivalence.

## Completion threshold

Complete only when the invariant is either rejected with falsification evidence or accepted with deterministic cases that prevent conformity-only denial without preventing evidence-based denial.
