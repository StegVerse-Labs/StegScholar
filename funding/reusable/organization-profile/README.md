# StegVerse-Labs Funding Organization Profile

## Purpose

This is the reusable source for grant and funding applications. It is deliberately evidence-bounded: unknown legal, registration, staffing, and financial facts remain marked `UNVERIFIED` and must not be converted into affirmative application claims without an inspectable source record.

## Public identity

- Working organization name: `StegVerse-Labs`
- GitHub organization: `StegVerse-Labs`
- Primary work: open protocols, validators, reference implementations, research artifacts, and governance controls for secure communications, governed AI agents, continuity, provenance, admissibility, and recoverable authority.
- Application authority: `StegVerse-Labs/StegScholar`
- Protected-disclosure authority: `StegVerse-Labs/StegPatents`
- Budget and financial-execution authority: `StegVerse-Labs/StegFinCo`
- Post-award deliverables authority: `StegVerse-Labs/StegOps-Deliverables`

## Reusable capability statement

StegVerse-Labs develops interoperable technical and governance components intended to make consequential digital actions more inspectable, constrained, recoverable, and resistant to silent authority drift. The ecosystem includes secure communication concepts, commit-time permission decisions, continuity and provenance records, fail-closed validators, task and claim registries, research formalization, and evidence-preserving ingestion and custody patterns.

This statement describes the repository program and its intended capabilities. It does not establish production deployment, independent adoption, security certification, organizational eligibility, or commercial readiness.

## Research and engineering themes

1. Governed AI-agent execution and explicit decision rights.
2. Secure, resilient, transport-independent communications.
3. Continuity, provenance, chain of custody, and reconstructability.
4. Runtime admissibility and fail-closed control boundaries.
5. Open-source governance, contributor continuity, and evidence-based releases.
6. Governed research reuse and machine-verifiable validation.
7. Multilingual access and education for trustworthy digital infrastructure.

## Evidence rules

Application writers must cite repository paths, commit SHAs, workflow runs, artifacts, releases, publications, contracts, or signed institutional records. Repository names, README claims, generated plans, and chat history are not sufficient evidence of maturity or eligibility.

## Public-product maturity fields

Each proposed open-source product must have its own evidence crosswalk covering:

- canonical public repository and precise product boundary;
- license and copyright posture;
- implemented versus scaffolded components;
- deterministic tests and hosted workflow evidence;
- releases and dissemination;
- security and privacy posture;
- maintainers and contributors;
- independent users, deployments, forks, citations, pilots, or integrations;
- known limitations and unresolved risks.

## Use restrictions

- Do not state that StegVerse-Labs is a corporation, nonprofit, small business, educational institution, or registered federal applicant until evidence exists.
- Do not name a Principal Investigator as eligible until employment and residency requirements are verified for the specific solicitation.
- Do not publish protected technical details without a StegPatents disclosure classification.
- Do not submit a budget without StegFinCo approval.
- Do not describe an application as submitted, awarded, deployed, or activated without the corresponding receipt.
