## Title decision gate

The solicitation requires a title beginning with `PESOSE:` and identifying the track. The NSF AI-agent Dear Colleague Letter instructs responsive proposals to use `PESOSE / AI: Track #:`. Because the current concept may align with the AI-agent protocol priority, final title syntax is `REVIEW_REQUIRED` and must be confirmed by the named PI/AOR or documented NSF program guidance.

Candidate working title, not authorized for submission:

`PESOSE / AI: Track 1: Governed Protocols for Secure and Reconstructable AI-Agent Ecosystems`

Fallback solicitation-form title, not authorized for submission:

`PESOSE: Track 1: Governed Protocols for Secure and Reconstructable AI-Agent Ecosystems`

## Submission-readiness gate

This control matrix may report `SUBMISSION_READY` only when:

1. all nonconditional components above are complete and authority-approved;
2. conditional components are either complete or documented as not applicable;
3. the selected open-source anchor product, license, public pointer, user base and contributor base are verified;
4. three to five collaboration letters are accepted;
5. the applicant, PI and AOR are verified and registered;
6. the budget and justification are approved;
7. IP and disclosure review is complete;
8. the final PDF and system-entered proposal pass sponsor and organizational compliance checks; and
9. an authorized submission receipt is produced.
