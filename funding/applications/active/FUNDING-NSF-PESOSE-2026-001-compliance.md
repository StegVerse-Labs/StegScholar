# NSF PESOSE Track 1 Compliance and Page-Budget Map

## Solicitation

- Program: NSF 26-506, Pathways to Enable Secure Open-Source Ecosystems
- Track: 1 — Scoping and planning
- Deadline: 2026-09-01 at 5:00 p.m. submitting organization's local time
- Project Description maximum: 7 pages
- Maximum budget: USD 300,000 for up to one year
- Letter of intent: not required
- Preliminary proposal: not required

## Required title form

`PESOSE: Track 1: Governed Open-Source Infrastructure for Recoverable AI Agent and Secure Communication Ecosystems`

## Project Summary controls

The Project Summary must contain:

1. overview;
2. intellectual merit;
3. broader impacts;
4. final line beginning `Keywords:` with two to five prioritized semicolon-separated terms.

Proposed final line:

`Keywords: AI agent ecosystems; cybersecurity; open-source governance; secure communications; provenance`

## Seven-page Project Description allocation

| Section | Maximum pages | Required content |
|---|---:|---|
| Existing product and national need | 1.0 | Product pointer through citation, problem, current context, societal/national importance |
| Product status and team qualification | 1.0 | Development/testing model, dissemination, users, contributors, team capability and limitations |
| Ecosystem discovery | 1.0 | Need testing, user and developer discovery, OSE justification, falsifiable go/no-go criteria |
| Organization and governance | 1.0 | Governance, licensing, integration, security/privacy safeguards, metrics |
| Risk analysis and security plan | 1.0 | Safety, privacy, identity, secure modification/release, chain of custody, recovery |
| Community building | 0.75 | Workshops, research networks, onboarding, contributor recruitment and retention |
| Milestones, evaluation and sustainability vision | 1.25 | Actionable milestones, metrics, long-term managing-organization and funding vision |

Total: 7.0 pages.

## Solicitation-specific mandatory content

- Existing publicly available open-source product pointer through inline citation and References Cited.
- Current development and testing model.
- Dissemination methods.
- User base.
- Contributor base.
- Problem being addressed.
- Strong team qualification justification.
- Ecosystem discovery strategy.
- Governance and licensing planning.
- Continuous development and deployment planning.
- Security, safety and privacy risk planning.
- Identity management, secure release and chain-of-custody planning.
- Community-building plan.
- Specific milestones and evaluation plan.
- Long-term sustainability vision.

## Required supplementary documents

- Minimum three and maximum five letters of collaboration from independent current users or contributors.
- List of Project Personnel, Collaborators, and Partner Organizations in required table form.
- Standard NSF senior/key personnel documents under the PAPPG in effect at submission.
- Data management and sharing plan.
- Postdoctoral mentoring plan only if postdoctoral researchers are included.

## Budget controls

- Total request must not exceed USD 300,000.
- Project duration must not exceed one year.
- Voluntary committed cost sharing is prohibited.
- Mandatory I-Corps for PESOSE experiential activities must be included using current NSF supplemental budget guidance.
- No budget may be marked approved outside StegVerse-Labs/StegFinCo authority.

## Submission readiness rule

The application remains blocked until every mandatory solicitation item has a source paragraph, evidence reference, responsible owner, and completed compliance review. Formatting presence alone does not establish eligibility, merit, or submission authority.
