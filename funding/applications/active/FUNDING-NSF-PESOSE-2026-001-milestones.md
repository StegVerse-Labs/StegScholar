## Evaluation questions

1. Is there verified demand from users beyond the founding maintainer or repository family?
2. Is the anchor product sufficiently implemented, licensed, tested, documented and maintainable to support ecosystem formation?
3. Can contributors participate without receiving unbounded execution or release authority?
4. Are governance, security, privacy, provenance and chain-of-custody mechanisms understandable and usable by independent participants?
5. Can the proposed managing organization sustain maintenance, incident response, succession and community operations?
6. Does an open ecosystem provide a clear advantage over a single-vendor or research-only continuation path?

## Provisional success thresholds

Thresholds must be preregistered after baseline discovery and may not be rewritten solely to manufacture a successful outcome. Initial planning targets are:

- at least 20 structured discovery participants across at least four stakeholder classes;
- at least five organizations or independent projects providing concrete use, contribution, validation or integration feedback;
- three to five qualifying independent current-user or contributor collaboration letters for the proposal;
- one precise licensed anchor product with an evidence-backed maturity inventory;
- documented governance, security, privacy, licensing, sustainability and succession plans;
- independent review finding no unresolved critical blocker to a Track 2 proposal;
- a final decision classified as `PROCEED`, `REVISE`, or `NO_GO`, with evidence and dissent preserved.

These targets are planning assumptions, not claims of current participation.

## Fail-closed conditions

The Track 1 plan must recommend `NO_GO` or `REVISE` rather than Track 2 progression when any of the following remains unresolved:

- no legally usable open-source license;
- no precise implemented product boundary;
- insufficient independent user or contributor demand;
- critical security or privacy risks without owned remediation;
- governance that cannot survive maintainer loss or conflict;
- funding or organizational structure that depends on unverifiable assumptions;
- protected IP boundaries incompatible with the proposed open ecosystem;
- inability to produce inspectable evidence for milestones and decisions.
