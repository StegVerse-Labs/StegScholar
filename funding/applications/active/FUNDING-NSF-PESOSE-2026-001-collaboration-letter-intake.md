# PESOSE Collaboration-Letter Intake

## Control state

- Application ID: `FUNDING-NSF-PESOSE-2026-001`
- Requirement: minimum three and maximum five letters from third-party current users and/or contributors of the selected open-source product.
- State: `BLOCKED — QUALIFYING LETTER AUTHORS NOT YET VERIFIED`
- Owner: StegScholar funding lane for intake; each independent author retains authorship and factual authority.
- Submission prohibition: StegScholar may not fabricate, ghost-author, backdate, or characterize a person or organization as a current user or contributor without evidence and the author’s approval.

## Qualification rules

Each proposed letter must satisfy all of the following:

1. The author is independent of the proposing team.
2. The author or represented organization is a current user or contributor of the selected open-source product.
3. The relationship to the product is supported by inspectable evidence.
4. The letter describes the author’s actual contribution or use.
5. The letter describes a credible intended continuing contribution to the proposed ecosystem.
6. The letter contains no unsupported product-maturity, adoption, security, or performance claim.
7. The author reviews and authorizes the final signed letter.

A facilities-infrastructure letter is additionally required if the proposed ecosystem depends on facilities provided by the proposing or another organization after the award.

## Evidence intake fields

For each candidate author, record in a protected or appropriately classified evidence surface:

- full name and role;
- organization and contact channel;
- relationship to the selected product;
- immutable contribution, issue, release, deployment, usage, or evaluation evidence;
- independence from the proposing team;
- expected continuing contribution;
- conflicts of interest;
- permission to reference the relationship in the proposal;
- signed final letter and receipt.

Personal contact information must not be committed to a public repository unless the author explicitly authorizes publication.

## Author request brief

The request sent to a qualified author must ask the author to describe, in their own words:

- how they currently use or contribute to the selected product;
- why the proposed ecosystem matters to their work or community;
- how they expect to continue contributing during or after the planning project; and
- any limitations or conditions on that participation.

The request must not ask the author to endorse claims they cannot independently support.

## Release condition

The collaboration-letter gate passes only when three to five qualifying, author-approved letters are stored in the authorized submission system or protected application evidence store, each with a durable intake receipt and a verified relationship to the selected product.
