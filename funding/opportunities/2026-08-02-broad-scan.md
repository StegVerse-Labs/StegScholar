# Funding Opportunity Scan — 2026-08-02

## Scope

Current public opportunities were screened for fit with StegVerse projects involving trustworthy AI, governed agents, cybersecurity, privacy, secure communications, open-source infrastructure, research data, education, digital rights, health, and environmental systems.

## Priority 1 — begin now

### NSF 26-506 — Pathways to Enable Secure Open-Source Ecosystems (PESOSE), Track 1

- Sponsor: U.S. National Science Foundation
- Deadline: 2026-09-01, 5:00 p.m. submitting organization local time
- Maximum Track 1 award: USD 300,000
- Eligible organization classes include U.S. institutions of higher education, qualifying U.S. nonprofit research or educational organizations, U.S.-based for-profit organizations including small businesses, state and local governments, and Tribal Nations.
- Core fit: StegCore, StegAgents, StegTalk, StegID, Continuity, ingestion engines, admissibility tooling, governance protocols, secure supply-chain controls, and the wider StegVerse open-source ecosystem.
- Best track: Track 1, because current need is ecosystem scoping, governance, licensing, security/privacy planning, contributor and user discovery, sustainability, and organizational formation rather than a claim of established broad adoption.
- Submission gates: legal applicant identity; U.S. ownership/control evidence; employee PI; legal right to work; active UEI and SAM.gov registration; public open-source product pointer; institutional submission authority.
- State: DRAFTING, blocked from submission by applicant-registration evidence.
- Canonical application: `funding/applications/active/FUNDING-NSF-PESOSE-2026-001.json`
- Draft narrative: `funding/applications/active/FUNDING-NSF-PESOSE-2026-001-concept.md`

## Priority 2 — partner-led or later-cycle

### NSF 25-515 — Security, Privacy, and Trust in Cyberspace (SaTC 2.0)

- Target date surfaced: 2026-09-28.
- Fit: StegTalk, StegCore, StegID, continuity receipts, runtime refusal, admissibility and reconstructability research.
- Constraint: PI eligibility generally requires a qualifying research or teaching appointment at an eligible U.S. institution. Treat as an academic-partner opportunity unless a qualifying appointment and lead institution are established.
- State: ELIGIBILITY_REVIEW.

### NSF 26-512 — Unlocking Dataset Value for AI-Enabled Scientific Discovery

- Fit: master-records, Data-Continuation, StegDB, governed research commons, provenance-preserving datasets, and AI-ready scientific evidence packages.
- Constraint: requires a qualifying submitting organization and a dataset-centered scientific discovery case with community demand.
- State: GO_NO_GO_REVIEW.

### NSF 26-508 — TechAccess: AI-Ready America

- Fit: StegLearn, StegScholar, StegAgents, multilingual access, AI workforce readiness, and public-interest deployment.
- Constraint: likely requires a consortium and regional or institutional partners rather than a single repository ecosystem.
- State: PARTNER_DISCOVERY.

### U.S. Department of State — Global Digital Threat Lab, DFOP0019364

- Deadline: 2026-08-13.
- Expected single award: approximately USD 2,960,039.
- Fit: StegTalk secure communications, StegWhisper, privacy safeguards, spyware-resistance concepts, digital-threat evidence, and human-rights-aligned governance.
- Constraint: compressed deadline, large operational scope, international implementation expectations, and applicant eligibility/capacity evidence. Do not submit without a qualified nonprofit, educational, international, or for-profit lead and delivery partners.
- State: REVIEW_REQUIRED; not selected as the first application.

## Not selected

- Closed opportunities were excluded even where technical fit was strong.
- Defense SBIR topics requiring specialized antennas, hypersonic platforms, counter-swarm hardware, or other capabilities not evidenced in current StegVerse repositories were excluded.
- Opportunities requiring already-established institutional partnerships were retained only as partner-led candidates.

## Decision

Begin NSF PESOSE Track 1 immediately. Preserve the other opportunities as durable candidates, but do not represent StegVerse as eligible or submission-ready until legal identity, ownership, PI employment, UEI, SAM.gov, and submission authority are evidenced.
